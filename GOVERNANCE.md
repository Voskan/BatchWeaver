# Governance

This document describes how BatchWeaver is currently governed. The model is
intentionally lightweight and will evolve as the project and its community grow.

## Roles

### Founder and lead maintainer

Voskan Voskanyan (GitHub: [@Voskan](https://github.com/Voskan)) is the project
founder and current lead maintainer. The lead maintainer is responsible for the
overall technical direction, release authority, and final decisions where
consensus is not reached.

### Maintainers

Maintainers have write access and review and merge contributions. At this early
stage there is a single maintainer; additional maintainers may be added as the
project grows.

### Contributors

Anyone who submits issues, pull requests, documentation, or reviews is a
contributor. Contributions are governed by [CONTRIBUTING.md](CONTRIBUTING.md).

## Decision process

- Routine changes are decided through normal pull-request review.
- Significant or cross-cutting technical decisions are documented as Architecture
  Decision Records under [docs/adr/](docs/adr/).
- When consensus cannot be reached, the lead maintainer makes the final decision.

## Architecture Decision Records

Major architectural decisions must be captured as ADRs so that the reasoning is
preserved for future contributors. See [docs/adr/README.md](docs/adr/README.md).

## Security decisions

Security reports are handled privately per [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md). The lead
maintainer coordinates disclosure and fixes.

## Releases

The lead maintainer holds release authority until a broader maintainer group is
established. Release and versioning conventions are described in
[CHANGELOG.md](CHANGELOG.md) and the roadmap.

## Growing the maintainer team

As the project matures, contributors with a sustained track record of
high-quality work may be invited to become maintainers. The criteria and process
will be formalized in a future revision of this document.
